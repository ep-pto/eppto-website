Bylaws

Our Bylaws

Our bylaws set out how the Eagle Pointe Elementary PTO is organized and governed, the roles and responsibilities of the board members, how decisions are made, how meetings are run, and how funds are handled. They are reviewed periodically and approved by the board. Anyone in the Eagle Pointe community is welcome to read them.
