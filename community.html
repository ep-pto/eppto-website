What’s Happening Around Eagle Pointe

A roundup of events happening in the surrounding community, plus minutes from CAPE meetings that affect our PTO and school.

Community Events

  • Oct 1

    Dr. Joe Sweeney: Create the Space to Find Their Place

    6:30 PM provider fair, 7:00 PM presentation · PHS-CC, 24120 W Fort Beggs Dr

    Ten things parents can do from kindergarten through senior year. Presented by the Plainfield Parent Community Network and District 202. Snacks provided, and free childcare with registration. See the flier (opens in a new tab) for the registration links.

  • Oct 2–4

    Friends of Fountaindale Book Sale

    Fountaindale Public Library · 300 W Briarcliff Road, Bolingbrook

    Public sale Saturday, October 3, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and Sunday, October 4, noon to 3:00 PM. Members get in early on Friday, October 2, 3:00 to 5:00 PM, and Saturday, October 3, 9:00 to 10:00 AM. Cash or check only. See the flier (opens in a new tab).

  • Oct 17

    Bilingual Magic Show

    Plainfield Public Library

    A bilingual magic show for families. Sign-up required through the library.

  • Oct 19

    Parent Group 101

    5:00 PM

    A CAPE session for parent group leaders and anyone curious about getting involved.

  • Oct 24

    Halloween Walk

    Spooktacular 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM · Plainfield Public Library

    The library’s Halloween walk, with its spooktacular running from 11:00 to 1:00.

  • Oct 24

    Halloween Spooktacular

    11:00 AM – 2:00 PM · YMCA

    Bounce houses, crafts, and games. The YMCA is looking for small businesses and personal trunks for trunk or treat.
