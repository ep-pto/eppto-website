Donate

Support Eagle Pointe

Every donation, big or small, goes straight back to our students, teachers, and classrooms — and donations are tax deductable. You can give money to support PTO programs, or donate items we’re currently collecting. Thank you for helping our school community!

Donate Money

Monetary donations help fund events, classroom supplies, teacher appreciation, and student programs throughout the year. Contributions are handled securely through our giving page.

Donate Items

We’re always looking for donations of the items below. Drop them off any time at the school front office — no need to call ahead. If you have something you think we could use that isn’t listed, get in touch and ask.

  • Teacher Coffee Bar

    • K-cups (regular and decaf)

  • Holiday Shop

    • Wrapping paper
    • Holiday poly mailers
    • Ribbon, bows, and gift tags
    • Scotch tape

  • Teacher Appreciation

    • Check back soon!

Questions?

Not sure what’s needed right now, or want to arrange a larger donation? Reach out and a board member will be glad to help.
