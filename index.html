Welcome to the Eagle Pointe Elementary PTO

We are a parent-teacher organization dedicated to supporting the students, staff, and families of Eagle Pointe Elementary. Through events, volunteering, and community building, we work together to enrich the educational experience for every child. Whether you’re a returning family or new to Eagle Pointe, we’re glad you’re here!

Get involved, stay connected, or make a donation to help us make this year the best one yet!
