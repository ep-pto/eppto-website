Become a Member

Why Join?

Membership is the simplest way to support everything the PTO does. Your dues go directly toward events, classroom support, and programs for Eagle Pointe students. Membership is open to every parent, guardian, and staff member.

Joining doesn’t commit you to volunteering or attending meetings. It just means you’re behind what we do. (Though we’d love to see you at both!).

Members get first choice on volunteer sign-ups, a voice at the school, an opportunity to connect with other Eagle Pointe families, and additional chances to earn fun raffle prizes!

What Your Membership Supports

  • Classroom supplies and teacher appreciation
  • Field trips, Kindergarten shirts, student folders, 5th Grade Celebration
  • Building improvements
  • School-wide events and family nights
  • Support for Specials Programs

Curious where the money goes? Our budget is published for everyone to see.

Ready to Join?

Signing up takes just a couple of minutes. Membership runs for the current school year.
