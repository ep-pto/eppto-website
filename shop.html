Shop

Shop & Support Eagle Pointe

A few ways to shop that also support PTO programs: subscribe to popcorn for the year, buy popcorn for the month, load a Spirit Shop balance for your student, or pick up some Eagle Pointe gear.

Eagle Popcorn Express

We have a popcorn machine! Subscribe for the year and a bag comes home with your student once a month (10 bags total) — no need to remember to pay each time, and it works out to less per bag than paying month-to-month.

  • $10.00 per student for the full year (1 bag/month, 10 bags total)
  • Subscriptions must be purchased by August 26th.
  • After August 26th, students can still get popcorn month-to-month at the regular rate of $1.50/bag, paid through Cheddar Up in the days leading up to each Popcorn Day — see the non-subscription link below for the current month.

Not subscribing? You can still buy this month’s bag for $1.50 (limit 1 per student). August orders are due August 27th by 10:00 AM.

Monthly Spirit Shop

Once a month, students can shop the Spirit Shop during lunch — spirit sticks, bracelets, fidgets, vintage tees, and more. Load a balance online ahead of time and volunteers will call your student up to the table on shop day.

Only load what you want spent that day — the shop closes online the morning of, and this link is updated each month for that month’s shop.

Load August Spirit Shop Balance (opens in a new tab)

Eagle Pointe Spirit Wear

Show your Eagle Pointe pride! Our spirit wear shop offers t-shirts, hoodies, and more for students, families, and staff. Every purchase helps support PTO programs and events throughout the year.

Questions?

Not sure about an order, a subscription, or a Spirit Shop balance? Reach out and a board member will be glad to help.
