Volunteer

Lend a Hand

Every PTO event runs on volunteer time, with parents, guardians, and grandparents giving an hour here and there. You don’t need experience, and you don’t need to commit to the whole year. If you can show up, we can use your help. And if you’d like a bigger role, joining a committee is the way to take on ongoing work across the year — it’s all volunteering, just at a level that fits you.

Ways to Help

  • Event-day help: setup, games, food tables, cleanup
  • Classroom party helpers
  • Spirit store sales and popcorn poppers
  • Behind-the-scenes prep you can do on your own schedule
  • Join a committee for an ongoing role — fundraising, events, communications, and more

Not sure what fits? Check this month’s events to see what’s coming up, or ask us and we’ll find you a spot.
